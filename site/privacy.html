Privacy

Step Competition · last updated 12 November 2026

Step Competition lets you race friends on daily steps, whatever watch or phone you use. This page says what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, so you can sign in.
  • The name you pick, shown to the other players.
  • Your daily step totals, one number per day, for roughly the last month.
  • Which device your steps come from (for example "Garmin" or "Fitbit"), shown next to your name.
  • The competitions you start or join.

That's all. We don't read your location, heart rate, workouts, sleep or any other health data, and we don't use ad trackers or analytics.

Where steps come from

Apple Health. With your permission, the app reads only your step count from Apple Health, on your phone, and uploads daily totals. You can turn this off at any time in the Health app.

Fitbit (through Google). If you connect Fitbit, you sign in with Google and allow read-only access to your activity data. Our server keeps the access Google gives us and uses it only to fetch your daily step totals. You can disconnect at any time from your Google account's security settings.

Who can see it

Your name, device and daily step totals are visible to other players of Step Competition, so leaderboards work. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

Health and fitness data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone except the service that hosts our database (Supabase), which stores it for us.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your data while you have an account. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your email, name, step history and any Fitbit connection.

Children

Step Competition isn't meant for children under 13.

Contact

Questions, or want your data deleted? Send feedback from the TestFlight app, and we'll reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
